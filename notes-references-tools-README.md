# The Collected — Notes, References & Tools

Supplementary notes for the project. Concept and process are covered in the main README and the *Dreaming in Collections* document; this file adds the theoretical framing, the key artwork reference, the full tool list, and my own thinking about the work.

---

## Reflections

*In my own words — the questions and observations behind the project.*

**Does a generated artwork have a medium?** This is the question underneath everything. The project moves the output across states — image, print, resin, video, simulation — partly to ask whether any of them is really its medium, or whether a generated work is something else.

In the video, the character is titled **collected** over and over. I named it almost artificially, imitating the whole process of AI and machine learning — the way a model labels, sorts, and repeats. The naming is part of the work, not a caption on top of it.

I wanted to present **a life and a time represented by colour and algorithm**. The cinematic X-ray visual gives us a chance to look at the mundane differently — to see an ordinary thing through a strange register and find it changed.

**AI creates ghosts.** It is far from reality in a deeper sense. I used the movements in the generated videos as an opportunity for change — a way to shift how we think and perceive. There are some game aesthetics in the work, but they are not decoration: they carry the narrative and support the collected's body and its emotional presence.

## Tools

### Models and training
- **Stable Diffusion 1.5**, **U-Net**, **DreamBooth LoRA**, **IP-Adapter**, **InstructPix2Pix** — image generation, style/structure conditioning, and the paired-image transformation model
- **WGAN** (trained in MATLAB) — trained on my own cemetery photographs; drives the video
- **Model merging** — custom weight-merging scripts to fuse the two museum models into one
- **Python**, **PyCharm**, **Hugging Face** training scripts

### Video and generation platforms
- **ComfyUI** — node-based generation, including **WAN** video
- **WAN** (via ComfyUI and Hugging Face) — video generation
- **LTX** — video generation
- **Kling** — video generation
- **ElevenLabs** — video generation and audio / sound
- **image-to-audio** — sound generated by converting a still from the green video into audio

### Simulation and real-time (in this repo)
- **MuJoCo** + **Gymnasium** + **Stable-Baselines3 (PPO)** — the world model that teaches the collected to move (`/simulation`)
- **three.js** — the real-time environment for the collected (`/three-js`)
- **TouchDesigner** — custom visuals: slit-scans, matrix effects, compositing (`/touchdesigner`)

### Fabrication and materials
- Resin 3D printing, dye-sublimation heatpress, UV flatbed printing
- Clear resin, recycled PLA, latex, leather, salvaged screen surfaces

All models were trained and merged locally; generation platforms above were used for the moving-image work.
